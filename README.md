# NeuroLance Hub
### AI Projects & Services Freelancing Platform

**Module:** PUSL2019 – Information Management & Retrieval

**Project Type:** Group Coursework

## Project Overview

NeuroLance Hub is an AI-focused freelancing platform designed to connect clients and organisations with freelancers offering Artificial Intelligence projects and professional services.

The platform facilitates freelancer registration, client project management, proposal submissions, project assignments, payment tracking, reviews and administrative reporting.

## Core Modules

1. Freelancer Management
2. Client & Project Management
3. Proposal & Assignment Management
4. Payment & Review Management
5. Administration & Reporting
